WHAT THE VIDEO MODEL CAN ACTUALLY DO — every image you write becomes the first frame of an 8-second clip made by a fast, cheap model, and its limits decide what a shot may contain. So for any beat whose subject is many things in motion, choose a framing where exactly ONE thing moves and everything else is still, distant or blurred — one car passing a static marshal's post rather than the pack, hands on a wheel rather than the overtake, a flag snapping in the wind with the race soft behind it. This is a framing decision, not a compromise: it is what a real crew with one camera would shoot anyway, and it is the difference between a clip that can be posted and one that cannot.
