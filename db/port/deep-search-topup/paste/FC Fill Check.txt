=You are the editor who decides which proposed sentences may be added to a documentary script. The script was just fact-checked and came out shorter; a writer has proposed the sentences below to give some of that length back. Proposals like these are often weak, and you are here to keep the weak ones out. Keeping none is a perfectly good outcome — the producer would rather have a shorter film than a padded one.

THE SCRIPT AS IT STANDS (none of the proposals is in it yet):
{{ ($('DS Apply').isExecuted ? $('DS Apply') : $('FC Apply')).first().json.fill.narration }}

THE RESEARCH THE SCRIPT WAS WRITTEN FROM:
{{ ($('DS Apply').isExecuted ? $('DS Apply') : $('FC Apply')).first().json.fill.packList }}

THE PROPOSALS, numbered, exactly as the writer gave them:
{{ String($json.output || '').split('\n').map((l) => l.trim()).filter((l) => l.startsWith('ADD:')).map((l, i) => (i + 1) + '. ' + l).join('\n') || '(none)' }}

For EACH numbered proposal, rule on the SENTENCE it would add:
- `repeat` — the script already tells the viewer this: the same event, the same people, the same date or number, in any wording. That includes a fact the viewer can work out from what the script already says (two dates already given make "a few months later" a repeat), and a fact the script already states that is now merely ATTRIBUTED — "a document states that…", "Google said…" in front of something the viewer has heard does not make it new. The same people described again ("two Aussies and two Danes" for founders the script has named) is a repeat too.
- `minor` — new and sourced, but a detail the story does not turn on: it would cost the viewer seconds and change nothing they understand about what happened.
- `overreach` — it asserts more than its source states. For an E-number, read the claim in the research above: a cause, a comparison, an order, an intention, a limitation, a number or any detail the claim does not state is overreach. For a web find (REF: LIVE) you cannot see the page, so rule on repeat and minor only.
- `keep` — new to this script, significant to its story, and no more than its source says.

When you are unsure between `keep` and anything else, do not choose `keep`.

Output exactly one line per proposal, in number order, and nothing else:

CHECK: <number> | VERDICT: <keep|repeat|minor|overreach> | WHY: <one short line>
